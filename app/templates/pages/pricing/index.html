questions

The short version.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. Lifetime and Supporter are single payments — $5 or $9.99, once. Nothing renews, so there's nothing to cancel.

What's the difference between Lifetime and Supporter?

Lifetime unlocks every Pro feature — video backgrounds, effects, the verified badge, image hosting, encrypted chats. Supporter includes all of that and adds gold-only cosmetics: the gold check with halo, animated gold username, sparkle trail, gold border, gold cursors, exclusive fonts and early access to new features.

Do I have to use the effects I unlock?

No. Every perk is a toggle in your dashboard. Don't want the halo, the sparkle trail or a particular animation? Turn it off. Upgrades add options; they never force a look.

What does a free page look like?

A full misa.lol page: your name, unlimited links, the music player and analytics. Free pages show a small misa.lol footer badge, which Lifetime removes.
